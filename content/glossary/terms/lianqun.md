A **lianqun** (链群, *chain group*; Haier's English *ecosystem micro-community*, EMC) is a self-organizing association of [micro-enterprises](xiaowei.md) responsible together for a particular end user's outcome. The term joins 链 (*chain*: coverage of the complete service or production sequence) and 群 (*group*: joint commitment). Haier introduced it in its 2019 restructuring, replacing much of the earlier platform vocabulary.

## Membership and purpose

In the model, members join voluntarily around an outcome rather than being assigned by a headquarters department. A unit can refuse an unsuitable target, proposed value allocation or participating group; without the needed consent and capabilities the proposed group may not form. This is a **governing design claim**, not evidence that any particular business has enacted it.

The model distinguishes an *experience chain group* (体验链群), responsible for the relationship with the user and the value they experience, from a *solution/creation chain group* (创单链群), responsible for producing the solution. These are complementary functions of the intended user-facing assembly, not necessarily two independent legal organizations.

## How the group coordinates

Its [chain-group contract](lianqun-heyue.md) joins participants to a single user outcome, target and value-sharing arrangement. [Binglian](binglian.md), the parallel-wiring analogy, describes the accountability: units may perform work sequentially but are not meant to earn merely by obtaining the next internal unit's sign-off. [Value-added sharing](zengzhi-fenxiang.md) specifies how surplus over the committed target is distributed. The actual contract, authority and observed user receipt still determine whether a particular group works this way.

## Distinctions and historical scope

A lianqun is not simply a value chain (a descriptive sequence), department (parent-appointed organizational unit), cross-functional project team (members retaining departmental authority), or consortium whose members primarily contract with each other. Its distinctive claim is *shared responsibility for an end-user result through a jointly accepted agreement*. The term should not be silently substituted for earlier **platform** arrangements: descriptions from Haier's 2013, 2016 and 2019 reorganizations describe different structures. Distinguish the applicable period before comparing them.
