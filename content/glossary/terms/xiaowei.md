A xiaowei (小微, *xiǎo wēi*, “micro-enterprise”) is the basic operating unit in Haier's [Rendanheyi](ren-dan-he-yi.md) model: a small group, often described as roughly ten to twenty people, organized around a user contract and its own economic results. The size is a historical description, not what makes the unit an enterprise.

**The distinction from a department is accountability and authority.** A department works against an allocated budget and a manager's directions. In the model, a xiaowei is responsible for its own results, holds [decision, personnel and distribution rights](sanquan.md), can choose collaborators and allocate its proceeds, and may fail when the contracted user value is not delivered. Calling an unchanged budget-funded department a micro-enterprise does not give it those rights or that exposure.

Three types answer to different users:

- **Market micro-enterprise** (市场小微): serves an end user directly and holds a product or user relationship.
- **Node micro-enterprise** (节点小微): provides design, manufacturing, logistics or another service to other micro-enterprises, whose customers remain downstream.
- **Incubating micro-enterprise** (孵化小微): seeks a new user or value proposition the firm has not yet served.

Its operating instruments include a [value-adjustment contract](duidu.md) with targets, a [value-added statement](gongying-zengzhi-biao.md), [user-derived compensation](ren-dan-chou.md) and contestable leadership through [bidding for an order](qiangdan.md) and [mutual selection](guanbing-huxuan.md). These mechanisms have distinct roles: a counted task is not a user's accepted value, and a nominal autonomy declaration is not full authority.

A xiaowei is not automatically a team, project pod, conventional subsidiary or franchise. It operates inside a larger firm but is intended to bear meaningful user-facing risk and decision rights. Haier's reported several thousand micro-enterprises after the 2013–2014 restructuring show the scale of its decomposition, not whether any individual unit met this test. Counts, rights and chain-group arrangements vary by period; current examples require their own dated evidence.
