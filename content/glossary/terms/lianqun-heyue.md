A **lianqun heyue** (链群合约, *chain-group contract*) is the agreement through which the members of a [lianqun](lianqun.md) commit to one end-user outcome. The model contrasts this shared agreement with a chain of internal purchasing and payment contracts.

## Commitment and division of value

The proposed contract identifies the user outcome, a challenging benchmarked target, the participating [micro-enterprises](xiaowei.md), the contribution and allocation terms, and how value above the target is shared. [Zengzhi fenxiang](zengzhi-fenxiang.md) is the distinct value-added-sharing mechanism; this entry defines the **common contracting relation**, not a second compensation theory.

Under the model, members share both upside and exposure to an unmet group target. A unit that has met its own local specification cannot treat that fact alone as satisfying the group contract if the user outcome was missed. The [parallel-wiring](binglian.md) analogy explains why the common user, rather than an intermediate department, is intended to determine accountability. This arrangement can reduce incentives for local optimization at the user's expense; **it does not logically guarantee that the delivered product will succeed**.

## Voluntary participation and authority

The intended agreement is accepted, not imposed: a unit may decline an unreachable target, an unacceptable division or unsuitable collaborators. A declined or unsigned offer is not a received chain-group contract. Likewise, a model template or recorded membership claim does not by itself establish legal signature, enforceability, actual risk sharing, independent customer acceptance or payment. These require the corresponding source and recipient evidence.

## Boundaries

A chain-group contract is not a conventional internal service-level agreement, transfer price, parent-imposed performance target or partnership whose primary counterparties are the participating units. Its distinct purpose is to align those units around an identified **end user**. Where real contracts retain internal payers or managerial override, record that difference rather than asserting the ideal form was implemented.
